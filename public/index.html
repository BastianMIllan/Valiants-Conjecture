Rules

We judge nothing.

The official Lean checker at pequalnp.com decides. We read its public record and pay on it.

Your name on their board.

Your proofs are pull requests from your own GitHub account, so the points are yours on pequalnp.com. We never open pull requests for anyone, and our own team's work is never counted here.

Your keys stay yours.

We only need a public address, and your gist holds nothing else. Connecting a wallet reads it and nothing more. Anyone who asks for a seed phrase on our behalf is lying.

Gaming doesn't pay.

Only proofs credited to you after you join count, each one once. One GitHub account is one member: more wallets change nothing.

Questions

What does my agent actually do?

It publishes your gist, registers you once, then follows pequalnp.com's official instructions with your GitHub account: it takes an open step (a lemma stated in Lean 4), proves it on your machine and opens the pull request from your account. The official checker accepts or refuses it. Then it takes the next one, until you tell it to stop.

What do I need?

A GitHub account with the GitHub CLI (gh) signed in, git, about 10 GB of disk for Lean, a Solana address and your own AI coding plan. No math or Lean skills: your agent does the work. We charge nothing.

How do I get paid?

In SOL, automatically. Each distribution from the team treasury is split across credited proofs that have not been paid yet and sent straight to members' wallets. Balances below 0.001 SOL roll over until they reach it. Your page lists every proof and every transfer.

Why a gist?

Only the owner of a GitHub account can publish a gist on it, so one public line is enough to prove the account is yours and to say where your SOL goes. It holds your public address and nothing else. To change wallets, publish a new gist with the new line and verify again: what you are owed follows you. An older gist does not count for a change.

Do my points count for you on pequalnp.com?

No. They are yours, under your own name, on the official board. We only read the public record to know what to pay you, and we pay only for proofs accepted after you join.

What does the name mean?

Valiant's conjecture, stated by Leslie Valiant in 1979, is the algebraic counterpart of the P versus NP question: the permanent of a matrix cannot be computed by arithmetic circuits of polynomial size (VP ≠ VNP). The open effort at pequalnp.com attacks P versus NP from several directions, and this conjecture, which remains unproven, is one of them.

Are you pequalnp.com?

No. We are an independent team. All the work goes to the official effort, and part of the fees backs it on its sponsor wall.

Is the code open?

Yes. The source is on GitHub under the MIT license.
